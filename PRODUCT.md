# AUMOREX transport

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Undecided. This phase is branding and website planning only. No implementation, booking engine or payment integration requested.

## Users
Primary: professionals needing fast, dedicated door-to-door car transport, including transport industry partners. Secondary: private customers, particularly holidaymakers moving their cars between Ireland/the UK and Spain/Portugal. Intermediate private jobs also help fleet positioning; that internal commercial rationale is not public copy.

## Product Purpose
Build trust through a portfolio and generate quote requests. Jobs are agreed directly through human contact; the site does not confirm availability or take bookings.

## Positioning
Dedicated open transport on a single-car platform/trailer. Own drivers and fleet, supported by a broad carrier network. Romania-based with a focus on western Europe.

## Operating Context
Principal corridor: Ireland, UK, France, Spain and Portugal. Other European routes, including Switzerland, are possible on enquiry. The principal driver, the founder's father, has more than 25 years of transport experience. The founder is a secondary driver and handles documentation/legislation. This is driver experience, not a claim about company age.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Open transport; standard vehicle load up to 2,200 kg. Up to 2,500 kg at extra cost, subject to suitable equipment and confirmed operational feasibility.
- Complete non-running vehicles can be winched aboard. Suitability to be confirmed case by case; do not imply all damaged vehicles qualify.
- Dedicated, rapid, door-to-door service. No fixed pickup or transit-time promise provided.
- Own fleet and partner carriers. Do not imply every job uses an own-fleet vehicle.
- User commits to quote-enquiry response within three hours, 24/7. Distinguish initial response from a fully confirmed quotation or departure.
- Quote fields: contact name, telephone, email, vehicle, collection/delivery locations and preferred date. Proposed optional fields: company, running condition, weight, flexibility, notes.
- English primary; German secondary. Team speaks English, German, Romanian and Hungarian.

## Brand Commitments
Name: AUMOREX transport. No existing identity. Professional, modern and trustworthy. User likes Dan Car Logistics but wants a less crowded site, reflecting a focused service offering and more space for testimonials. That competitor-like direction is explicit; do not introduce an unrelated visual concept tournament.

## Product Principles
1. Lead with the dedicated single-car service and western European routes.
2. Support trust with real drivers, actual transport photographs and attributable client feedback.
3. Keep the quote request short and make personal contact easy.
4. Make clear which facts are confirmed and which depend on the individual job.
5. Use one consistent identity across web, social media, vehicles and documents.
